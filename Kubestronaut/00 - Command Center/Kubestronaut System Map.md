## Flow of evidence

Daily learning creates notes, labs, recall, and weak areas.

Labs create command practice and troubleshooting evidence.

Troubleshooting creates diagnostic skill evidence.

Mock exams create readiness evidence.

Weekly and monthly reviews consolidate evidence into certification trackers.

Certification trackers support booking decisions only when evidence is sufficient.
