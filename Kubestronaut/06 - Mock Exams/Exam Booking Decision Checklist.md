## Exam booking decision checklist

Use this checklist before booking any certification exam.

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] Official exam objectives checked against current CNCF/Linux Foundation sources #kubestronaut #exam-readiness
- [ ] Certification tracker is up to date #kubestronaut #exam-readiness
- [ ] Mock exam notes exist for recent attempts #kubestronaut #mock-exam
- [ ] Weak areas are recorded in [[Weak Area Register]] #kubestronaut #review
- [ ] Review tasks exist for missed concepts #kubestronaut #review
- [ ] Labs are completed or explicitly not required for this exam type #kubestronaut #lab
- [ ] Troubleshooting drills are complete where relevant #kubestronaut #troubleshooting
- [ ] Time management is acceptable #kubestronaut #exam-readiness
- [ ] No prerequisite blocker remains #kubestronaut #certification

## Decision outcome

| Decision | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Book | Evidence supports readiness | Schedule exam and create final review plan |
| Delay | Gaps remain | Add weak-area tasks and choose a reassessment date |
| Blocked | Requirement missing | Resolve prerequisite or administrative blocker first |

## Final review plan after booking

- [ ] Schedule final review sessions #kubestronaut #review
- [ ] Re-run weak-area drills #kubestronaut #review
- [ ] Complete one final timed practice session #kubestronaut #mock-exam
- [ ] Confirm exam environment requirements #kubestronaut #exam-readiness
- [ ] Confirm exam date and time #kubestronaut #certification

## Safety note

Do not store ID documents, account passwords, exam portal credentials, tokens, kubeconfigs, or private keys in Obsidian.
