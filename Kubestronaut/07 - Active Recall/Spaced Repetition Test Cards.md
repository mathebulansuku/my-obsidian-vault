What does the Kubernetes scheduler do?
?
It selects suitable nodes for unscheduled Pods according to resource requirements, constraints, and scheduling policies.

What is a Kubernetes Pod?
?
A Pod is the smallest deployable Kubernetes workload object and can contain one or more containers that share networking and storage context.

Why should Kubernetes Secrets not be pasted into Obsidian?
?
Because notes are not a secret manager and may sync, index, or expose sensitive credentials outside controlled access boundaries.

What does `kubectl auth can-i` check?
?
It checks whether the current user or service account is authorized to perform a specific Kubernetes action.
