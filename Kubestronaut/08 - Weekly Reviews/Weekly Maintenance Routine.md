## Weekly maintenance routine

Run this after the weekly review or at the end of the last study session of the week.

## Step 1 — Learning notes

- Check incomplete daily notes in [[Maintenance Dashboard]].
- Mark only truly completed sessions as completed.
- Move weak areas into [[Weak Area Register]].

## Step 2 — Labs

- Update lab status and actual_minutes.
- Mark repeat_required when you could not complete the lab independently.
- Link labs to relevant concept notes and troubleshooting notes.

## Step 3 — Troubleshooting

- Review incidents with low confidence.
- Add diagnostic commands that were actually useful.
- Create flashcards for repeated failure modes.

## Step 4 — Knowledge base

- Promote repeated daily learning topics into concept notes.
- Update low-confidence concept notes.
- Add links from maps to newly created concepts.

## Step 5 — Exam readiness

- Check [[Mock Exam Dashboard]].
- Confirm mock follow-up tasks are complete.
- Update certification tracker only when evidence exists.

## Step 6 — Safety and cleanup

- Remove any accidentally pasted secrets or sensitive values.
- Close obsolete tasks.
- Keep dashboards focused on action.
